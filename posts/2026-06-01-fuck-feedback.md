---
title: Fuck feedback
date: 2026-06-01
---
You walk into a board meeting with a failed product. Everyone has feedback. Here is what you should do with it: fuck it. Seriously.

For anything zero to one, feedback is a dangerous fallacy, even from your customers. Customers own problems, not solutions. Employees own processes, not solutions. Investors own equity, not solutions. Nobody in that room has built this thing before, so nobody knows.

The best thing you can do is free yourself from every outside voice.

Your life is zero to one too. Nobody has lived it before.
