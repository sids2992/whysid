---
title: A home robot is not an appliance
date: 2026-09-25
---
Every consumer robot demo is about chores. Think about it. We have (arguably) the most powerful technology ever built, and the most exciting thing we can show it doing is folding a T-shirt.

A home robot is not an appliance. It is the moment AI gets out of the two rectangles in your life and starts living in your world, in your context, with your stuff.

Imagine never buying groceries again. Imagine quitting an addiction because something at home does not let you slip. Imagine meals built around your health goals, not the menu.

Imagine everything. Most of it will probably be possible.
