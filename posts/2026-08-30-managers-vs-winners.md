---
title: Managers vs winners
date: 2026-08-30
---
For anything zero to one, a great manager will give you an amazing sense of security and almost certainly help you lose. A winner will look entropic, maybe even toxic.

In week one, the manager shows you a plan. The winner shows you three failed experiments. Zero to one means solving many unknowns in a short time. Managing assumes you already know most of the answers and can orchestrate your way to success.

If you are lucky, the winner finds the recipe and course-corrects. With a manager, you eventually realise you have to be the winner for them.

As a leader, you have two choices: pick a winner, or be one.
