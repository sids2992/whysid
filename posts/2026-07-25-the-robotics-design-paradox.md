---
title: The robotics design paradox
date: 2026-07-25
---
The strongest robot in the room is the one you should trust the least. At the World Robot Conference in Beijing last year, one demo boasted an 800 kg payload. The founder was certain it would take on the toughest jobs in factories and homes.

Then I thought about what happens when that robot hallucinates mid task. Language models make things up all the time; the cost is a wrong sentence. An 800 kg arm making something up next to a person is a very different kind of wrong.

This is the paradox every robot designer will live with. The properties that make a robot useful are the same ones that make it dangerous.
