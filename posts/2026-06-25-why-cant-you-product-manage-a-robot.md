---
title: Why can't you product-manage a robot?
date: 2026-06-25
slug: why-cant-you-product-manage-a-robot
---
You cannot product-manage a robot. Great product managers are super users of their own products. A super user lives on the edges of what the product can do and imagines a future the builders cannot. That is where good roadmaps come from.

There are close to zero super users of general purpose robots today. Nobody has lived with one long enough to be bored by it. So anyone promising to product-manage their way to a great robot is selling a skill that has nothing to work on yet.

Designers do not have this problem. They trace back to first principles and a few useful dogmas and make things up from there. Maybe you can product-design your way to a great robot.
