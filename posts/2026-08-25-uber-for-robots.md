---
title: Uber for robots
date: 2026-08-25
---
Uber was never competing with cabs. A few years back, someone walked me through sizing the Uber opportunity, and the real target was obvious: car ownership. Whatever Uber is today, that was the bet.

Now imagine Uber had existed in the early days of the car, when one cost a year's salary and only a few streets could take it. Most families would never have bought one.

That is where home robots are. Too expensive to own, too useful to ignore. So the first robot in your home will probably not be yours. It will be shared, scheduled, dispatched, and gone by evening.
