---
title: Metcalfe's law for home robots
date: 2026-10-15
slug: metcalfes-law-for-home-robots
---
Home robots have no network effect. The robot in your home gains nothing from the robot in mine. Fleet learning sounds like one, but it is not: a rival can buy the data and catch up. Metcalfe's law counts links between users, and today there are none.

There is one way to create them. Let a robot be taught by demonstration, and let the lesson travel. Your mother teaches her robot to make rajma the way she does. Yours downloads it. Every pair of homes that can teach each other becomes a link, and now the value grows with the square of the homes.

The moat is not the data. It is the recipe your mother left in the machine.
