---
title: The robot should look unsure
date: 2026-10-24
---
A person who is about to make a mistake usually shows it. They slow down, glance back, hesitate over the cup. The people around them read it and step in before anything breaks. Robots today give no such signal. They move with the same confidence whether they are right or about to drop the glass, so the human nearby learns to watch everything or nothing. A home robot that showed its own uncertainty, in its pace, its posture or a light, would let a family trust it in the moments it is sure and help it in the moments it is not. That is not a safety feature. It is the start of working together.
